## How to sign

Add yourself to the signature record (`CLA-SIGNERS.json`) in a pull request, with your name and
GitHub identity. A maintainer merges that entry, after which your contributions pass the CLA
gate.

The signature record is committed only to `main`; it cannot be edited by your own pull request.
