# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful community. We value clear, kind communication and
judge contributions on their merits.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Give actionable feedback; critique ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behaviour.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or spamming.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for enforcing this code. Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be
reported to the maintainer; they will be reviewed and handled appropriately. Maintainers may
remove or edit comments, issues, and contributions that do not align with this code of conduct,
and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for repeated or severe violations.
